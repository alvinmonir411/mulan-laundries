## 2. Business Claims & Statistics Audit

Every claim found on the existing site was recorded and analyzed for business validity and accuracy:

### Claim A: "Processing over 1,000,000 pounds per year"
- **Location:** `commercial-laundry.php` (Paragraph 1)
- **Text:** *"Processing over 1,000,000 pounds per year, Mulan Commercial Laundry New York operate under universal precautions in processing to adhere to JACHO and OSHA regulated standards."*
- **Assessment:** Demonstrates substantial commercial industrial capability. Must be preserved accurately as a historical business milestone.
- **Typographical Note:** The original site spells JCAHO as *"JACHO"*. The Joint Commission was formerly JCAHO. In the new build, this should be preserved or properly formatted while adhering strictly to OSHA and healthcare laundry standards.

### Claim B: "18 years old brand based in New York"
- **Location:** `about.php` (Paragraph 6)
- **Text:** *"We, the team of Mulan laundries, are proud to be a part of this 18 years old brand based in New York."*
- **Assessment:** Because the original site has copyright 2019, an 18-year claim in ~2019–2020 means the brand was established around 2001–2002. In 2026, it represents over two decades of experience.
- **Action:** Retain the verified phrase *"long-standing industry experience spanning over 18 years in New York"* without fabricating exact founding dates.

### Claim C: "Five different locations in New York City"
- **Location:** `about.php` (Paragraph 3 & 6)
- **Text:** *"We have five different locations in New York City to meet our customer needs."* and *"serve you anywhere at or around the five locations where we operate from, in New York City."*
- **Assessment:** While five operational distribution / drop locations across NYC boroughs are claimed, only the **Bronx** headquarters is explicitly printed on the website.
- **Action:** Preserve the operational reach across all 5 boroughs without inventing specific unverified street addresses for the other 4 satellite depots.

### Claim D: "Green Technology & Eco-friendly Detergents"
- **Location:** `index.html`, `wash-fold.php`
